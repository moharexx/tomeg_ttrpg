CRAFTING

Crafting is process of creating items, from mundane items, to magical artifacts, and even technological marvels.
You can Craft any Item from; Items and Costs, Curios, Consumable Items, and Vehicles, as long as it has a Market Price.
For this you will Spend Currency at a settlement which can provide, or your own Crafting Materials at hand.

The Mastery used is determined by the Item; Athletics for metalworking, Sleight for small precise things, Arcana for Magic, ect...
The Maximum Tier the Item can be is up to Mastery / 10
In a single workday (Max 8 Hours) you can Spend up to 10 × Mastery Currency in Crafting the Item.
Note: Crafting Materials at hand allow 10× faster Spending.

By having Essence Sustenance or Quick Recovery you can work for up to 16 Hours per day.
By having both, you can work 24 Hours every day...

Master Crafter

By Spending 5 XP, the Master Crafter Ability can be Unlocked, making Crafting easier and better.

Master Crafter:

  • Allows the Maximum Tier of Items Crafted to be Total XP / 50
  • Allows Spending up to 2 × Total XP in a single workday.
  • Provides a 10% Discount in Crafting, treating the Item as 10% less expensive.
  • Crafted Items are considered "Masterwork", allowing Innate Permanent Enchant Armaments.

Crafting Materials

Crafting Materials can be used for Crafting, and can impact the final product.
These Materials can range from mined minerals, ores, to exotic body parts of monsters, or even salvaged artifacts.
Crafting Materials already at hand are Spent 10× faster than regular Crafting.

The first property of Crafting Materials is Market Value, which tells the quantity and/or quality.
The Market Value determines how much it can contribute to Crafting an Item, and how much it's worth to sell.
Crafting Materials are consumed in parts, leaving leftover Market Value if it wasn't all used up.
Note to GM: Crafting Materials should be hard to sell at full price, as demand for such specific items are usually very low.

The second property are Tags, which determine what said Crafting Material can be used for.
These are the same Tags abilities use, for example; Offense, Defense, Heat Damage, Negation, ect...
The more specific a Tag is the more the Crafting Material should be usually worth.
Weapons accept the Offense Tag, while Armor the Defense, Negation Tags.
Ability Type Curios require at least 1 matching Tag, anything else is up to GM.

[Type] Damage Tag can be used for both Weapons and Armor;
If at least 20% of the Armament's Price is made of said Material, it replaces 1 Damage or Resistance Type.
This is the same as an Innate Permanent Imbue Armament.

Salvaging

Craftable Items can also be broken down back into Crafting Material(s).
Total Market Value should be equal to the Salvaged Item's, and should have 2-3 Tags, exact ruling up to GM.
This is done at 10× speed, the same speed as using Crafting Materials to Craft.
