Hulls

This is the base of the Vehicle, this section encompasses its size, weight, and Base Stats.
Hulls have a Tier Level, like many things, this is a general quality indicator, and the main scaling factor for the Hull's Stats.
A Hull has a Weight Class, these determine its Stats further.

Base Market Price of Hulls: 100 × Tier
Hulls also have 4 × Tier Armor VS All.
In the table below; Weight, Size, HP, STR, and Proxy scale with Tier Level.

  • If the Vehicle can Move then its AGI is Overridden; [Light/Medium/Heavy] [10/6/2] × Tier
  • The Vehicle benefits from the Pilot's Masteries, like Athletics, Acrobatics, Sleight, ect...
  • The Pilot can use any of their own Abilities as the Vehicle's.

Movement Types

If you want your Hull to be a Vehicle you probably want some way for it to move.
This determines the Speed and AGI of your Vehicle, choosing the AGI of the currently used Movement Type.
You can put multiple types of movement onto your Vehicle, alternatively you could put none for something stationary.
The Movement must be at least equal Tier to the Hull.

Industrial Grade Weapons

Industrial Grade (I.G.) Weapons are Weapons too big to be comfortably used personally, and must be Mounted to something or Wielded by a Vehicle.
These are mostly like regular Weapons, and have 8 Property Points, but there are some differences.
Using when not Mounted or not Wielded by a Vehicle gives Disadvantage, unless STR is at least 20 × Tier

Industrial Grade Weapon Stat
Market Price 100 × Tier
Weight 10Kg × Tier

There are 2 Types of Industrial Grade Weapons; Mounted and Wieldable.
These Types also interact differently with Melee and Ranged.

  • Mounted: These must be Mounted to a Vehicle, taking 10 × Tier PROXY from a Vehicle.
  • Wieldable: These will require to be Wielded by a Vehicle to be used, requiring Mech Arms.

Melee I.G. Weapons:

  • When Mounted; replace the Attack made on Vehicle Collisions.
  • I.G. Shield are also possible, but only Wieldable ones.

Ranged Mounted I.G. Weapons:

  • The Weapon has a Clip Size, 3 for Light, 2 for Medium, 1 for Heavy.
  • To fire, someone must use an Interact-Action (Bonus-Action)
  • When firing the Weapon, it makes an amount of Attack-Actions equal to the Clip Size.
  • Only the Slow version of the Reload Property can be used, which makes it so the Weapon must reload for 1 Round to fill its Clip.
  • The Aim Property cannot be used.

Industrial Grade Curios

Industrial Grade (I.G.) Curios are Special Curios, not designed to be personally used, and must be Mounted to a Hull.

Modifications

Hulls, I.G. Weapons and I.G. Curios can have a few special modifications.

Miniaturization:

  • With this the Hull, Movement Type, I.G. Weapon, or I.G. Curio can be much smaller and lighter.
  • For 2.5× Market Price the Weight and Size is reducible, Capped to 1/5 of Tier 1.
  • The AGI of the Vehicle gets Doubled!
  • A Miniaturized Hull only accepts Miniaturized Components.
  • Note: If you want to make an iron man suit, this is the way.
