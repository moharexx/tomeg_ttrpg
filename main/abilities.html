ABILITIES

Abilities are special actions and passive benefits, which require to be Unlocked.
Abilities canonically have an Essence Cost, we don't keep track of it as a Resource, assume you always have enough Essence.
When listing the Stats (like Damage) of Abilities you will see ESS, this is the Essence Cost, which scales with your Total XP.
Maximum ESS Of Abilities: Total XP / 100 (Rounded down, at least 1)

Casting Stat will be used to calculate the Stats of a lot of Spell Type Abilities, shortened to CS.
CS is equal to: INT | WILL | EGO

Basic Assumptions:

  • Spell Ability To-Hit Modifier: CS / 2
  • Skill Ability To-Hit Modifier: Same as Attack with the used Weapon (Includes Natural Weapons)
  • Touch Abilities have the same Reach as your Natural Melee Weapons.
  • Touch Abilities have the same To-Hit as Natural Melee Weapons, but if a Spell it can be the same as a Spell's.

Unlocking an Ability canonically makes you able to do more than the simple combat description of the Ability.
Like with a fire ability you also gain some control over fire itself, moving, reshaping, coloring, bending, ect...
These uses are called "tricks", these are less combat realated uses.
Example: With Fire Bolt you can conjure a small flame above your palm.

Ability Categories

Active Abilities:

  • Active Abilities are the most intuitive, you can simply use it on your Turn.
  • You can count Free-Action Active Abilities with a Duration as some kind of Passive.

On-Attack Abilities:

  • On-Attacks are used just before making a "Parent" Attack or Save, and can be made as Reactions too.
  • By default, even if the Parent is an AOE, the On-Attack will Target ONLY ONE Target!
  • When an On-Attack and the Parent both do the same Damage Type, unify it as one single Damage Instance.
  • If an On-Attack creates an Offspring Attack, you can put On-Attacks on that too.

Reactive Abilities:

  • These Abilities can only be used when some Trigger is met.
  • These can be used outside of Turn order, if you have the Actions to do so.
  • More information about Reactions.

Stance Abilities:

  • When using Stance Abilities you are put in a Stance, the Ability specifies what can Break or Trigger said Stance.
  • Breaking or Triggering a Stance Ends it.
  • You can only be in one Stance at a time.
  • You can Break your own Stance as a Free-Action.
  • Some Stances can be Stacked, allowing you to use the Stance again while already inside.
  • Note: Attack-Action, Defense-Action, Shoving, and Grappling Count as Abilities!
  • Using On-Attack Abilities don't Break the Stance!

Meta Abilities:

  • Meta Abilities are used to Modify different Abilities in special ways.
  • If the Modification is anything other than Potency, then the Meta-Ability's ESS must reach at least its "Parent".

Passive Abilities:

  • Passive Abilities provide some continuous effect, or allow new unique Actions.
  • Passives which have the Auto Tag will apply automatically if the Requirements are met.

PROXY

PROXY is a Stat which is used like a "current" rather than a regular resource, and is equal to INT + WILL + EGO
PROXY is usually used to hold control over Minion Entities, or Attune to some Curios.
If the Caster reaches 0 SP, they lose Control over all PROXY.

Ability Memorization

For 5 XP you can Unlock Memorizing Abilities, allowing you to Memorize 1 Ability.
You can only Memorize Abilities which don't have higher XP Costs than your INT Stat.
While you have an Ability Memorized you can use it, with all its Unlocks.

Memorizing Abilities:

  • During a Short-Rest you can Re-Memorize what Ability you have.
  • To Memorize an Ability you need to have someone who can use said Ability with you during the Short-Rest.
  • If it's a Spell you can Memorize from a written form (like a book), or a Magical Device which can Cast said Spell.
  • If you Memorize from a Magical Device you need an Arcana Check of 2 × Ability's XP Cost for a Success.
  • For Spells you don't need their Unlock Requirements, but for other Types you do.

Casting Memorized Abilities:

  • The ESS at which you Cast Memorized Abilities is INT / 10
  • If it's a Spell treat your CS as if it were equal to your INT Stat.

Instant Memorization (Non-Action):

  • If you Pinpoint an Ability being used, you can instantly try to Memorize said Ability.
  • You can Memorize an Ability you already have Unlocked even if it wasn't used in the moment. (niche use case)
  • Instant Memorization's Cooldown is Long-Rest, and can be Refreshed by Spending 1 Fate.

Empowered Casting:

  • You can use your Memorized Ability in an Empowered state, this has a Cooldown of a Short-Rest.
  • An Empowered Ability's Type becomes Innate and treats all your Main Stats as if they were equal to your INT Stat.
  • You can Empower a Passive Ability, putting it in an Empowered state for 1 Minute (20 Rounds).
  • You can Refresh the Cooldown by Spending 1 Fate, or using Instant Memorization.

Attached Meta-Abilities:

  • If you have written forms of Meta-Abilities, when you Memorize you can also Attach said Meta-Abilities to it.
  • When you use your Memorized Abilities, it will always use it with the Attached Meta-Abilities.
  • If some Meta-Ability is on Cooldown then you can't use your Memorized Ability.

A written Spell's Market Price is 10 × XP Cost, but may not be on sale, especially higher level Spells.
A Player Character may also write down or copy a Spell they have, this takes 100 × XP Cost / (INT + Arcana) Hours.

Flow State

For 5 XP you can Unlock the Flow State, allowing you to Unlock 1 Temporary Ability.
Entering the Flow State is a Non-Action Reaction and lasts for 1 Minute (20 Rounds).
The Ability's XP Cost mustn't be higher than your WILL, and its Requirements must be met.
You also gain all of the Ability's Unlocks for which you have the Requirement.
For this Ability treat all your Base Stats as if they were equal to your WILL, and its ESS as WILL / 10
Flow State's Cooldown is Short-Rest, Refreshed by Spending 1 Fate. (You can Stack multiple Flow States)

Empowered Casting:

  • While you have a Temporary Ability, you can use it once in an Empowered state, turning the Ability's Type to Innate.
  • When you choose a Passive Temporary Ability it's Empowered for the full Duration.

Ability Wishing

For 5 XP you can Unlock Ability Wishing, giving you 3 Wishes, replenishing after a Long-Rest.
If your EGO is at least the Ability's XP Cost, you can Wish that Ability, with all its Unlocks.
Wishing takes the same Action and Resource Costs as normally using the Ability does.
The GM may allow Wishes that are not normally replicable by an Ability.

Wished Abilities:

  • Treat all your Base Stats as if they were equal to your EGO Stat, the ESS as EGO / 10, and the Ability's Type as Innate.
  • You can put Meta-Abilities on the Wished Ability, and at 50 EGO you can put any Meta-Ability, even ones not Unlocked.
  • As a Non-Action Reaction you can Wish to Unlock a Passive or Meta-Ability for 1 Minute (20 Rounds).
  • Stances and Ability Durations don't last longer than 1 Minute (20 Rounds).
  • At 1000 EGO you can make Wishes at Half ESS without Spending a Wish.
  • You can Spend 1 Fate in place of a Wish.
