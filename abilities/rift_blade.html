Rift Blade

Requirements:

  • Skill: 20 Melee Weapon | 20 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Offense, Defense, Utility
  • Crowd Control
  • Main-Action

Description

You imbue your Melee Attack with a crack in space it self.
On-Hit the Target makes a Soul Save of 14 × ESS, or get Teleported 6 × ESS meters to a Pinpointed Position.
You can Teleport with the Target, and offset yourself from them by your Melee Reach
The Teleportation's Distance is measured from yourself.
Rift Blade's Teleported does NOT Activate Reactions, or Trigger Stances.

You could also just spend Main-Action + Bonus-Action to Teleport 6 × ESS + Melee Reach
