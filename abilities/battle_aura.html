Tags:

  • Defense
  • Self, Buff
  • Bonus-Action

Description

You can use this Ability when making the Switch-Action, allowing you to change your Armor.
You can either Simulate an Armor, or change the one you're currently Wearing.
The new Armor can have up to 6 Property Points, and Tier equal to your ESS.
If you change an Armor you're currently Wearing, the changes only last while Wearing it.
