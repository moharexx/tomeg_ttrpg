Aura Of Subsidy

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Melee, Static Sphere AOE, Negation, Buff
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action / Main-Action + Bonus-Action

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Aura Of Subsidy Stance.
While in Aura Of Subsidy Stance, you Subsidize those your favored Entities.
Chosen Entities (Excluding yourself) when entering the Radius gain Subsidy, or they can resist this with a Soul Save of 10 × ESS
Entities under Subsidy gain 2 × ESS Healing, Shielding, BUFFERS, and Negation.
On a Successful Save, the Target Entity is immune to this Aura for 1 Minute (20 Rounds)
The Subsidy from this Ability is only effective while the Target is within the Radius.

Stance Trigger (Main-Action + Bonus-Action):

  • Chosen Targets must Succeed a Soul Save of 14 × ESS, even those that have Save immunity from this Stance.
  • On a Failed Save: the Targets get the Subsidy with 2 × ESS Healing, Shielding, BUFFERS, and Negation until the End of your Next Turn.
  • On a Successful Save: nothing.

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
