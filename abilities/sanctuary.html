Sanctuary

Requirements:

  • Skill: 5 CS, (5 Abjuration | 10 Enchantment | 20 Anima)

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Touch, Negation, Buff, Unlock
  • Main-Action

Description

When used, you protect a Touched willing Entity giving them Sanctuary.
An Attacker must Succeed a Soul Save to be able to Target an Entity under Sancuary with anything harmful. (Any Offense Tagged Ability)
On a Fail the Attacker's Action is wasted.
If an Entity under Sanctuary attempts to directly harm anyone else, they lose the Sanctuary effect, and get the Staggered Condition.
An Entity can use a Bonus-Action to remove the Sanctuary from themselves.
"Anything harmful" is kinda up do GM, be logical please and reasonable. (pov: me when i cope)

Mass Sanctuary (10 XP):

  • You make this Ability into a Stance for enchanced effects.
  • Willing Entities Within the Radius (Excluding yourself) gain Sanctuary.
  • You don't gain any of the benefits from the Sanctuary, but must still follow its rules, or the Stance Breaks.
  • This Stance Breaks from: Using Abilities, Sanctuary Break Conditions
