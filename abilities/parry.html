Parry

  • Skill: 10 Melee Weapon | 10 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Negation, Debuff
  • Bonus-Action

Description

You ready yourself to Parry a single incoming Attack.
Till the End of your Next Turn, if there is an incoming Attack or Save, you can try to Parry it.
Roll Parry Strength against the Pre-Mitigated Damage, you need to Roll at least equal for a Success.
On a Success; you fully Negate the Attack from yourself, with all of its effects, and block the Flow of AOEs.
On a Failed; you are Hit by the Attack, or if it was a Save you Fail it.

Parry can only be used against Attacks and Saves with Damage, Hybrid Damage's weight (HP + SP) isn't doubled, Multi-Type Damages get added together.
If the Attack you Parried was Melee, the Target gains the Staggered Condition.
You can use the Parry Ability multiple times, when you do so you can Parry that amount of different Attacks.
This Ability can CRIT for 2× Parry Strength, the CRIT determining Dice is the same d10 used in Parry Strength, PARRY THE HEAVENS!!
