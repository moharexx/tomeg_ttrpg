Parry

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 Melee Weapon | 10 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Negation, Debuff
  • Bonus-Action

Description

You ready yourself to Parry a single incoming Attack.
Till the End of your Next Turn, if there is an incoming Attack, or a Save with Damage, you can try to Parry it.
Roll for Parry Strength against the Pre-Mitigated Damage, you need to Roll at least equal for a Success.
On a Success; you fully Negate the Attack from yourself, with all of its effects, and block the Flow of Flow AOEs.
On a Failed; you are Hit by the Attack, or if it was a Save you Fail it.

If you Successfully Parried a Melee Attack, the Target gets the Staggered Condition.
You can use Parry multiple times, when you do so you can Parry that amount of different Attacks.
This Ability can CRIT for 2× Parry Strength, the CRIT determining Dice is the same d10 used in Parry Strength, PARRY THE HEAVENS!!
