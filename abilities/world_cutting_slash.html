Description

"Phase; Scale of the Dragon; Recoil; Twin Meteors..."
"World Cutting Slash"

When using this Ability, you are put in World Cutting Slash Stance.
The Next Turn you can Trigger the World Cutting Slash, making a single Attack against a Pinpointed Target.
This Attack counts as Melee, it simply happens at the Target's Position.
Each point of Damage going into BUFFERs or Shielding Abilities count as 2 points of Damage.

The Stance Breaks when: Using Abilities, Moving, HD Decrease, Taking Damage
