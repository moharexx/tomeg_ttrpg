Heal More

Requirements:

  • Spell: 20 CS, (10 Transmutation | 20 Enchantment | 50 Anima)

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Ranged, Healing

Description

When used, you give out a total of 20 × ESS Healing between Pinpointed Targets in Range.
The Maximum Healing any Target can recieve is 4 × ESS

Potency Boost:

  • For each point of Potency, gain +5 Healing Pool, and +1 Max Healing.
