Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Thorns Stance.
While in Thorns Stance; every time you're Hit by a Melee Attack you can Attack back as a Free-Action Reaction, regardless of Reach.
This is a Melee Attack with 5 × ESS Static To-Hit, 5 × ESS Penetration, and 5 × ESS Physical Damage, but can use the Damage Type of any Wielded Melee Weapon.

The Stance Breaks only when entering any other Stance.

Note: On-Attack Abilities can be use as Reactions.
