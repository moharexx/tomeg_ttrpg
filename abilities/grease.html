Grease

Requirements:

  • Spell: 5 CS, (5 Conjuration | 10 Transmutation | 20 Enchantment)

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Offense, Utility, Unlock
  • Static Cube AOE, AGI Save, Heat Damage, Crowd Control
  • Main-Action

Description

When used, select a Point you can Pinpoint, and cover everything with magical grease in that Area.
All Targets must make an AGI Save of 8 × ESS, on a Fail they become covered in Grease, and get the Prone Condition.
Any Entity which Enters or Starts its Turn in the Grease must also make an AGI Save against the same effects.
On a Save Success, the Target doesn't have to make Saves for the next 1 Minute (20 Rounds) against that specific Grease Ability Instance.
The Grease is a Pseudo Material and sublimates into nothing when the Spell's Duration runs out.

When a Heat Based Attack Hits the Grease it quickly Ignites all the connected Grease and burns up.
Any Target covered in Grease gets dealt 4 × ESS Heat Damage as the Grease burns away.
Soaked Effect clears the Grease Effect.

Stable Grease (20 Transmutation | 20 Enchantment):

  • When Casting this Ability, you can make the Grease Non-Ignitable.
