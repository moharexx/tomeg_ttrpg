Overheal

Requirements:

  • 10 Enchantment | 10 Anima

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Action Cost -, + BUFFER
  • Action Cost +

Description

You make a Healing Ability turn excess Healing into a BUFFER, by increasing the Parent Ability's Cast Time.
If the Healing goes above the Target's Max HP, then they gain the remaining Healing as a BUFFER which Ends at the End of their Next Turn.
You increase the Cast Time by either adding a Bonus-Action, or changing a Bonus-Action into a Main-Action.
