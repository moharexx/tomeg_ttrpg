Life Link

Requirements:

  • Spell: 10 CS, (5 Transmutation | 10 Anima | 20 Enchantment)

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Ranged, Negation, Buff, Unlock
  • Bonus-Action / Continuous

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Life Link Stance.
When Entering Life Link Stance, you link your Life with a Pinpointed Willing Entity.
When you or the Target take Damage, the post-mitigation Damage gets halved, and split between the two of you as True Damage.
If the Damage is odd, you two can decide who gets which result.

One Way Channeling (20 Divination, 5 XP):

  • On each Damage instance, you can decide to channel the full post-mitigation Damage to the other Entity as True Damage.

The Stance Breaks only when the Target gets outside of the Stance's Range.
