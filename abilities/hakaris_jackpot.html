Hakari's Jackpot

Requirements:

  • Spell: 20 CS, (10 Abjuration | 20 Enchantment | 50 Anima)

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Buff
  • Bonus-Action

Description

You Roll a d10 to see if you CRIT, if you do, you hit the JACKPOT.
If you don't, you can still hit the JACKPOT by doing a CRIT with anything till the Start of your Next Turn.
If you hit the JACKPOT; till the End of your Next Turn, all Damage you recieve, both HP and SP, is Reduced by 10 × ESS

IN OTHER WORDS, IN THE NEXT ROUND FOLLOWING A JACKPOT, HAKARI IS EFFECTIVELY IMMORTAL.
