Holy Ground

Requirements:

  • Spell: 10 CS, (5 Anima | 10 Enchantment | 20 Evocation)

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Ranged, Static Sphere AOE, VIT Save, Radiant Damage
  • Main-Action / Continuous

Description

Within Range, in a choosen Sphere sacred light starts to emanate.
When using this Ability, you are put in Holy Ground Stance.
When Entering this Stance and in your subsequent Turns, Entities in the Radius must Succeed a VIT Save or take Damage.
Within the Radius of this Ability Bright-Light is emitted, and Low-Light in double the Radius, both the Bright and Low-Light are a Static AOE.

Overflow (Main-Action):

  • For the rest of the Stance's Duration, you can specify which Entities are Targeted by this Ability.

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
