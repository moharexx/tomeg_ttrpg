Absorb Element

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 CS, (5 Transmutation | 10 Evocation | 20 Anima)

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Negation, Healing
  • Bonus-Action

Description

You ready yourself to Absorb a single incoming Attack.
Until the End of your Next Turn, you can Absorb 4 × ESS HP Damage from a single Damage instance.
You can use This Ability multiple times, when you do so you can Absorb that amount of different Attacks.

Potency Boost:

  • For each 2 points of Potency you can Increase the Absorbtion Amount by 1.
