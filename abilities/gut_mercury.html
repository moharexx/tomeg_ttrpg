Gut Mercury

Requirements:

  • Spell: 10 CS, (5 Conjuration | 10 Enchantment | 20 Abjuration)

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Ranged, VIT Save, Bio Damage, Debuff, Crowd Control, Effect
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action

Description

When used, the Targets insides gets filled with mercury, slowing and harming them.
The Target Entity must succeed a VIT Save of 10 × ESS, or get the Soaked Effect.

Soaked Effect:

  • The Target's MS is Reduced by CS^(1/3)
  • The Target can repeat the Save at the End of their Turns, Ending the Effect on a Success.
  • On a Fail the Target takes 2 × ESS Bio Damage with 10 × ESS Bio-Pen
