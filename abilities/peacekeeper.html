Peacekeeper

Requirements:

  • Innate: 200 Total XP

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Self, Negation, Buff
  • 1 Minute (20 Rounds) / Continuous

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Peacekeeper Stance.
While in Peacekeeper Stance, Reduced To-Hit against you by 50 × ESS, Reduce incoming Damage Instances by 20 × ESS, and have 10 × ESS Bonus on Saves.
If you attempt to directly harm anyone else, you gain the Paralyzed Condition Until the End of your Next Turn, and the Stance Breaks.
Leaving this Stance takes ALL of your Actions on this Turn.

The Stance Breaks only when you attempt to directly harm anyone else.
