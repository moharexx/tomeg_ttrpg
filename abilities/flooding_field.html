Flooding Field

Requirements:

  • Spell: 10 CS, (5 Conjuration | 10 Transmutation | 20 Evocation)

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Ranged, Static Sphere AOE, Physical Damage, VIT Save, Crowd Control
  • Main-Action / Continuous

Description

Within Range, in a choosen Sphere the air becomes extremely humid, and small water bubbles will start to from.
When using this Ability, you are put in Flooding Field Stance.
When Entering this Stance and in your subsequent Turns, Entities in the Radius must Succeed a VIT Save or take Damage.
Targets Damaged by this Ability get the Soaked Effect.

Overflow (Main-Action):

  • For the rest of the Stance's Duration, the full AOE is filled with water.
  • Entities inside the water will be effected by Underwater Rules.
  • Once the Stance Ends, all the water made by this Ability will sublimate into nothing.

Soaked Effect:

  • An Entity can use a Main-Action to Remove this Effect from themselves.
  • This Soaked Effect End if the Ability Ends.

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
