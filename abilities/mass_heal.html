Mass Heal

  • 20 XP

  • Defense

  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action / Main-Action + Bonus-Action

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Mass Heal Stance.
On Stance Trigger, you give out a total of 100 × ESS Healing between Sensed Targets in Range.
The Maximum Healing any Target can recieve is 20 × ESS

Potency Boost:

  • For each point of Potency, gain +5 Healing Pool, and +1 Max Healing.

The Stance Breaks when: Using Abilities, Moving, HD Decrease, Taking Damage
