Black Flash

Requirements:

  • Innate: 20 WILL

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense, Defense
  • Buff
  • Bonus-Action / Continuous

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Black Flash Stance.
While in this Stance, whenever you score a CRIT, you gain 6 × ESS Potency.
This Potency can't be Spent on the CRIT itself, but can be for any other Action, till the End of your Next Turn.

The Stance only Breaks when entering any other Stance.
