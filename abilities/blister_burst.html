Blister Burst

Requirements:

  • Spell: 20 CS, (10 Enchantment | 20 Transmutation | 50 Conjuration)

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Ranged, Flow Sphere AOE, VIT Save, Bio Damage, Effect
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action

Description

When used, the Target Entity swells up with blisters and pops, nearby Entities get infected from the pus.
The Target Entity must Succeed a VIT Save of 10 × ESS, or take 2d6 × ESS Bio Damage.
If the Target Failed the VIT Save, an explosion with a Radius of 3 × ESS^(1/3) gets centered on the Target.
Entites other than the Target within the Radius take the same Damage on a Failed AGI Save of 14 × ESS.

Soaked Effect:

  • On Failing a Save from this Ability, the Target gets Soaked with water.
  • This Effect Ends after the Entity uses a Main-Action to remove it.
