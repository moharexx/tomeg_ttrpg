Description

When you Unlock this Ability, first you must design your Domain, only then can you use it.
When taking a Long-Rest you can design or re-design your Domain.
No Meta-Ability or Potency can be put onto a Domain, they are complex and customizable enough as it is...

This Ability is heavily WIP content, and might be wildly unbalanced.
Simple Domains and Domain Amplification are WIP #soon #swoon

Designing Domains works by assigning Property Points (PP) to different Stats, Effects, and interactions.
Some add PP, while others subtract, like how they work on Armaments.
A Domain can be at most 10 PP by the end of the creation process.

Cast-Time:

  • Bonus-Action: 2 PP
  • Main-Action: 0 PP
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action: -2 PP

SP Cost:

  • 0 × ESS : 0 PP
  • 5 × ESS : -2 PP
  • 10 × ESS : -4 PP

Cooldown:

  • None: 2 PP
  • Short-Rest: 0 PP
  • Long-Rest: -2 PP

Radius:

  • 2 × ESS^(1/3) : -2 PP
  • 5 × ESS^(1/3) : 0 PP
  • 10 × ESS^(1/3) : 2 PP
  • 20 × ESS^(1/3) : 4 PP

Border:

  • Solid (opaque): 0 PP Has (10 × ESS) HP
  • Inner Border Armor VS All: Up to 4 PP, Adds 4 × PP × ESS Armor VS All.
  • Outer Border Armor VS All: Same as Inner Border.
  • No Border: -2 PP

Extras:

  • Stays where Cast: 0 PP (Default)
  • Moves with Caster: 3 PP (Requires No Border)
  • Doesn't End when Caster is outside: 1 PP
  • Puts you in a Stance, and requires it for upkeep: -3 PP
  • Requires to announce and commit (uses Action-Cost) a Turn before: -2 PP
  • When it Ends, disables using any Ability till End of your Next Turn: -2 PP

Inner World 2 PP:

  • Makes the inner Radius of the Domain up to 50 × ESS meters. (WIP)

For Interactions you must choose from the Abilities you have Unlocked, and imbue them with Special Effects
These Special Interactions work only if the Ability is Cast at ESS equal or less than the Domain.
Note that the Attack-Action, Grapple, Shove, and Defense-Action count as Abilities too.

Potent Technique Up to 6 PP:

  • Adds PP × ESS bonus Potency to the selected Ability, one time, refreshing at the Start of your Turns.

Sure Hit Effect Up to 6 PP:

  • Gives PP × ESS Bonus To-Hit, or (PP × ESS) / 3 Bonus Save Difficulty.

True Target 2 PP:

  • Ignore Range and Sense requirements, if you're Aware of your Target, and they're inside the Domain, you can Target them.

Full Area Nuke Variable PP:

  • Makes the Ability Target everyone and everything in the Domain, you can exclude yourself.
  • 8 PP to make a Single Target into Full Area.
  • 6 PP to make a Line AOE into Full Area.
  • 4 PP to make any other AOE into Full Area.

Warped 1 PP:

  • Changes the Damage Type and/or inflicted Effect Type of the Ability.
  • Even if the Effect Type is changed, duration and what it does remain the same, only Effect Interactions change.

Inflict Effect 2 PP:

  • On-Hit inflicts an Effect; Heat | Cold | Soaked | Light | Darkness | Charm | Terror
  • This Effect lasts till the End of your Next Turn, and does nothing by itself.

Free Cast Variable PP:

  • 2 PP to remove the base Resource Cost of the Ability, if it has any.
  • 4 PP to remove a Bonus-Action from the Cost, one time, refreshing at the Start of your Turns.
  • 8 PP to remove a Main-Action from the Cost, one time, refreshing at the Start of your Turns.

Finisher -4 PP from other Special Effects:

  • When you use your Finisher, your Domain Ends.
  • This can't make the Ability cost less than 0 PP, it only discounts its other Special Effects.
  • You can only have 1 Finisher.
