Signature Trick

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 Melee Weapon | 10 Ballistic | 10 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Utility
  • Self, Buff
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action / Continuous

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Signature Trick Stance.
When getting into this Stance, choose an On-Attack or Meta-Ability you have Unlocked, which Costs a Bonus-Action.
While in this Stance, you can use this On-Attack as a Free-Action, this has a Cooldown of 1 Minute (20 Rounds)

The Stance Breaks only when entering any other Stance.
