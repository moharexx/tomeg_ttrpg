Fighting Style

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense, Defense
  • Self, Buff
  • Free-Action

Description

With this Ability you can make your body act like Melee Weapons and Shields, creating Simulated Natural Weapons.
Simulated Weapons can have Max Property Points, and must be Wielded like any other Weapon.

When you use the Switch-Action, you can change the Simulated Armaments.
Casting this Ability counts as a Switch-Action, and Costs a Bonus-Action if Switch-Action is a Bonus-Action.
Simulated Melee Weapons cannot have the Throw Property, and cannot use the Weapon Throw Ability.

Finger Guns (50 Martial Arts, 20 XP):

  • You can Simulate Ranged and Throw Weapons, and can use the Weapon Throw Ability.
  • The Reload and Slow-Reload Property separately retain their state between Weapon Switches.
  • For Throwing you make the Ranged Attack as normal, and your Weapon remains Wielded.
