Imbue Armament

Requirements:

  • Innate: 20 WILL
  • Spell: 10 CS, (5 Enchantment | 10 Transmutation | 20 Anima)

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Utility
  • Touch, Buff
  • Bonus-Action

Description

When you use this Ability, you change an Armament's Damage or Resistance Types.
For each Damage or Resistance Type an Armament has choose an Imbuement.
A Weapon's Armor-Pen Stat will turn into the new Damage Types' Penetration.
This Ability must be Cast at least at the Armament's Tier or it will Fail!

If Cast as Innate, the Ability can only Target currently Wielded and Worn Items, and only Ends when Un-Wielded or taken off.

Simple Imbuement (Weapons):

  • List of Damage Types: Physical | Heat | Cold | Bio | Demat | Thunder | Electric | Psychic | Radiant | Necrotic
  • When Physical is chosen, you must decide at Cast to use either Bludgeoning, Slashing, or Piercing.
  • When Electric is chosen, it will NOT Maximize its Damage against Soaked or Airborne Targets.
  • When Radiant or Necrotic is chosen, it will deal ONLY either the HP or the SP part of the Damage, decided at Cast.

True Imbuement (Weapons):

  • List of Damage Types: Electric | Radiant | Necrotic
  • Electric Damage is Maximized against Soaked and Airborne Targets.
  • Radiant and Necrotic are Hybrid Damage Types, which go both into HP and SP.
  • Choose 1 Drawback:
  • 1. The Weapon loses 4 × ESS Armor-Pen. (Must have at least that much Base Armor-Pen for this!)
  • 2. Either add a Bonus-Action, or turn a Bonus-Action into a Main-Action when using any Ability which deals Weapon Damage Type.
  • Note: The Attack-Action counts as an Ability!

Resistance Imbument (Armors):

  • List of Resistance Types: Physical | Heat | Cold | Bio | Demat | Thunder | Electric | Psychic | Radiant | Necrotic
  • Change the Resistance Type into the chosen one.

COMBO:

  • This Ability can be used as a Main-Action to Target up to 3 Items instead of 1.
  • When you use the Switch-Action you can Cast this as a Free-Action Reaction on your Armaments.
  • When you Cast Enchant Armament, you can Cast this as a Free-Action Reaction on one of the same Armaments.
