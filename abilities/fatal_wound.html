Fatal Wound

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 Melee Weapon

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Physical Damage
  • Bonus-Action

Description

You make your Melee Attack Wound your Target.
On a Hit you inflict the Wound Effect.

Wound Effect:

  • In the Caster's Next Turn the Target takes (1d8 × ESS) Physical Executing Damage, with 10 × ESS Armor-Pen.
  • This Effect Ends after Dealing its Damage.
