Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Starsign 124 Stance.
While in Starsign 124 Stance; at the Start of Your Turns you roll a d10 for a Random Damage Type.
This new Damage Type only effect's your Melee Weapons.
Damage Types other than Electric | Radiant | Necrotic gain Bonus 2 × ESS Damage and To-Hit.
Damage Type Table: Physical | Heat | Cold | Bio | Demat | Thunder | Electric | Psychic | Radiant | Necrotic

Stance Trigger (Main-Action):

  • The Stance Ends, and Until the End of your Next Turn, keep your current Damage type and double the Damage and To-Hit Bonuses.
  • Damage Types without the Buffs gain the Buff.

The Stance Breaks only when entering any other Stance.
