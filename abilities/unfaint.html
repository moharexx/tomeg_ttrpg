Unfaint

Requirements:

  • Spell: 5 CS, (5 Enchantment | 10 Anima | 20 Transmutation)

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Touch, Buff
  • Bonus-Action

Description

When used, you Touch an Entity and Remove the Unconscious Condition.
If the Unconscious Condition is caused by an Ability then this Unfaint attempts a Save against the Ability's Save.
The Target is also immune to falling Unconscious from the Dying Condition for the Duration.
Note: The Dying condition still effects the Target.
