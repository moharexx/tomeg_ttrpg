Description

You use the Treat Wounds Action as a Main-Action, rather than the regular 1 Minute Cast Time.
The ESS this Ability is Cast at is Modifier / 10 (at least 1)

Additionally, you can make Surgeries with just a Main-Action, but 4× Difficulty.
This has no Cooldown.
