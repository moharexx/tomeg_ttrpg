Doomed Divison

Requirements:

  • Spell: 10 CS, (5 Anima | 10 Abjuration | 20 Enchantment)

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Ranged, Static Sphere AOE, VIT Save, Necrotic Damage, Debuff, Effect
  • Main-Action / Continuous

Description

Within Range, in a choosen Sphere, dark orbs of energy start flickering.
When using this Ability, you are put in Doomed Divison Stance.
When Entering this Stance and in your subsequent Turns, Entities in the Radius must Succeed a VIT Save or take Damage.

Overflow (Main-Action):

  • For the rest of the Stance's Duration, Damaged Entities will get the Darkness Effect.

Darkness Effect:

  • All Healing, Shielding, and BUFFER recieved is Reduced by 6 × ESS
  • This Effect lasts until the End of the Target's Next Turn.

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
