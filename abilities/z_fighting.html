Description

When using this Ability, you conjure an object inside a Pinpointed Entity, or overlay the Entity onto itself.
The Target must make a VIT Save of 10 × ESS or take 2d6 × ESS Bio Damage and get the Dazed Condition until the End of their Next Turn.
