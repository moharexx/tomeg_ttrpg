Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Warlord Ward Stance.
When Entering Warlord Ward Stance, you can Reduce a single HP Damage instance by 10 × ESS
The Stance Trigger can only be used after you Reduced a Damage Instance with this Stance.
On Stance Trigger: Enhance your Melee Attack-Action, giving it 5 × ESS Bonus To-Hit and Damage, the Type of which is the same as the Damage Reduced.

The Stance Breaks when: Using Abilities, HD Decrease
