Initiative Surge

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 Acrobatics | 20 INIT

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Utility, Cooldown
  • Buff, Action Economy
  • Free-Action, HD

Description

You lock in, Spending up to (10 × ESS) HD to increase your INIT for that Round.
The amount of INIT (and Rolled Initiative) you gain for that Round is equal to the HD Spent.
Note: Some features can lock your Rolled Initiative to 0, this does NOT bypass that.
