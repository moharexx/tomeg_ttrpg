Description

You make an Ability into a Reaction by increasing its Cast Time.
You increase the Cast Time by either adding a Bonus-Action, or changing a Bonus-Action into a Main-Action.
If the Cast Time of the Parent Ability is multiple Rounds then this Meta-Ability cannot be used!
