Geppo

Requirements:

  • Skill: 20 Athletics | 20 Acrobatics | 20 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Utility
  • Mobility

Description

You can pseudo Fly by jumping midair, allowing you to use movement midair.
You can do the Move-Action and any other Movement in any direction, like up or down.
You can choose to not Fall at the End of your Turn, remaining in the air.

At the Start of your Turns you must decide if you want to use Geppo, this is a Free-Action.
You can use Geppo up to (Athletics | Acrobatics | Martial Arts) / 10 times.
Geppo uses are restocked when the Airborne Condition Ends on you.

You have the Airborne Condition when Flying with this Ability.
You can still get the Prone Condition, imagined as a loss of balance.
If you have a Movement Impairing Condition you cannot use this Ability.
