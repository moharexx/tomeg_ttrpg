Star Strike

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 Melee Weapon | 10 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Melee, Weapon Damage
  • Free-Action / Trigger

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Star Strike Stance.
When you make any Single Target Melee Attack, you can Trigger this Stance.
On Stance Trigger: Enhance the Reach of said Melee Attack by 4 × ESS^(1/3)

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
