Strike Zone

Requirements:

  • Skill: 20 Melee Weapon | 20 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Melee, Weapon Damage
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action / Continuous

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Strike Zone Stance.
When an Entity Enters or starts Moving inside your Melee Reach, you can make an Attack-Action against it as a Free-Action.
When you do so, you can't Attack the same Target again with this Stance, while the Stance is Active.
When the Attack-Action would cost you only a Bonus-Action, then make the Attack with Advantage.

The Stance Breaks when: Moving
