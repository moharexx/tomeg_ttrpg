Aura Of Gravity

Requirements:

  • Spell: 5 CS, (5 Transmutation | 10 Enchantment | 20 Anima)

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Melee, Static Sphere AOE, STR Save, Debuff, Crowd Control
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action / Main-Action + Bonus-Action

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Aura Of Gravity Stance.
While in Aura Of Gravity Stance, you warp Gravity around yourself.
Chosen Entities when entering the Radius must Succeed a STR Save of 6 × ESS or get the Dazed Condition.
On a Successful Save, the Target Entity is immune to this Aura for 1 Minute (20 Rounds)
The Dazed Condition from this Ability lasts for 1 Minute (20 Rounds), and is only effective while the Target is within the Radius.

Stance Trigger (Main-Action + Bonus-Action):

  • Chosen Targets must Succeed a STR Save of 10 × ESS, even those that have Save immunity from this Stance.
  • On a Failed Save: the Targets get the Prone Condition, and the Stunned Condition until the End of Their Next Turn.
  • On a Successful Save: nothing.

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
