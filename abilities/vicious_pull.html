Vicious Pull

Requirements:

  • Spell: 10 CS, (5 Enchantment | 10 Evocation | 20 Conjuration)

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Ranged, STR Save, Debuff, Crowd Control
  • Main-Action

Description

When used, you Pull a Pinpointed Entity within Range towards yourself.
The Target gets Pulled 3 × ESS^(1/3) meters on a Failed STR Save of 8 × ESS
Additionally, the Target can also get the Prone Condition.
