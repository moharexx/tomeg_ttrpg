Moving Fortress

Requirements:

  • Innate: 50 VIT
  • Skill: 10 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Self, Negation, Debuff
  • Bonus-Action / Continuous

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Moving Fortress Stance.
While in Moving Fortress Stance, MS is Halved, but Reduce All incoming Damage Instances by 6 × ESS and make STR and VIT Save with Advantage.

The Stance Breaks when: HD Decrease, Taking Damage
