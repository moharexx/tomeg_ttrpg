Terror Entity

Requirements:

  • Spell: 5 CS, (5 Enchantment | 10 Anima | 20 Divination)

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Targeting, Soul Save, Effect, Crowd Control
  • Main-Action

Description

When used, you Terrify a Pinpointed Entity or more.
You have Targeting Points equal to 12 × ESS, you can Spend Targeting Points on Entities to Target them.
The Soul Save's Difficulty is equal to the Targeting Points Spent on the Target.
On a Failed Save the Target gain the Terror Effect.

Terror Effect:

  • Applies Disadvantage on Attacks against the Caster, and cannot willfully move closer to them.
  • This Effect Ends if the Caster gets the Dying Condition or Dies.
  • The Terror Effect last for the Ability's Duration.
  • Entities can repeat the Soul Save at the End of their Turns, Ending the Terror Effect on a Success.

This Ability can also have social interactions, like the Intimidate Action, making opponents flee or surrender.
Exact effects up to GM.
