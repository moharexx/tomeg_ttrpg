Quick Draw

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 Melee Weapon | 10 Ballistic | 10 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Variable Damage
  • Bonus-Action

Description

When using this Ability, you will be able to use the Attack-Action as a Free-Action once on your Next Turn.
This Ability can only be used if you haven't used an Offense Tagged Ability on that Turn. (This includes Quick Draw itself!)
If an Offense Tagged Ability is used in that Turn, then this Ability is Cancelled, and you DON'T gain back the Action Cost.
Note: Readying an Offensive Tagged Ability is by itself an Offensive Tagged Ability!

If the Attack-Action would only cost you a Bonus-Action, you make the Free-Attack-Action with Advantage.
This Ability cannot be used outside of your Turn, for example; as a Reaction, because like... that would be very weird.

COMBO:

  • The Free-Action Attack can be immediately used after you use a Reactive Ability like Opportunity Attack, or something Triggers your Stance.
