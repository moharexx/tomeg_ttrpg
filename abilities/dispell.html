Tags:

  • Utility
  • Ranged, Negation
  • Main-Action

Description

When used, you Target something magical (like a Spell, Magical Item, or similar...), and attempt to Dispell it.
Roll (1d10 + 5) × ESS against the Dispell Difficulty, if you Roll at least equal you Succeed.

Dispell Difficulty of a Spell is based off its Base Cast Time, ESS, and Potency; (+6 per Bonus-Action, +10 per Main-Action) × ESS + Potency
If the Base Cast Time of the Spell is multi Round, then its Dispell Difficulty is (20 × ESS) + Potency
For Permanent Effects the Dispell Difficulty is 40 × (ESS | Tier)

By using this as a Reaction (Example: Ready), it can be used as a Counter Spell.
Dispelling anything made by yourself is a Free-Action, and has 0 Difficulty.

Potency Boost:

  • For each Point of Potency you gain +1 Dispell Strength.
