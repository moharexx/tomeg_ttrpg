Dispell

Requirements:

  • spell: 20 CS, (10 Abjuration | 20 Anima | 50 Enchantment)

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Utility
  • Ranged, Negation
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action

Description

When used, you Target something magical (like a Spell, Magical Item, or similar...), and attempt to Dispell it.
Roll Dispell Strength against the Dispell Difficulty, if you Roll at least equal you Succeed.

Dispell Difficulty for Spells, Effects and such (uses ESS): (10 × ESS) + Potency
Dispell Difficulty for Magical Items, and such (uses Tier): 40 × Tier

If you use this Ability as a Reaction (Example: Ready), it can be used as a Counter Spell.
Dispelling anything made by the Caster themselves is a Free-Action, and has 0 Difficulty.

Potency Boost:

  • For each Point of Potency you gain +1 Dispell Strength.
