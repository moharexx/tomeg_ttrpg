Aura Of Protection

Requirements:

  • Spell: 5 CS, (5 Enchantment | 10 Abjuration | 20 Transmutation)

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Melee, Static Sphere AOE, Negation, Buff
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action / Main-Action + Bonus-Action

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Aura Of Protection Stance.
While in Aura Of Protection Stance, you Protect those that are closest to you.
Chosen Entities (Excluding yourself) when entering the Radius gain Protection, or they can resist this with a Soul Save of 10 × ESS
Entities under Protection gain 1 × ESS Armor VS All and Save Bonus, To-Hit against them is Reduced by 2 × ESS
On a Successful Save, the Target Entity is immune to this Aura for 1 Minute (20 Rounds)
The Protection from this Ability is only effective while the Target is within the Radius.

Stance Trigger (Main-Action + Bonus-Action):

  • Chosen Targets must Succeed a Soul Save of 14 × ESS, even those that have Save immunity from this Stance.
  • On a Failed Save: the Targets get Protection with 4 × ESS Armor VS All and Save Bonus, 8 × ESS To-Hit Reduction until the End of your Next Turn.
  • On a Successful Save: nothing.

Stance Trigger (Main-Action + Bonus-Action):

  • Chosen Targets must Succeed a Soul Save of 14 × ESS, even those that have Save immunity from this Stance.

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
