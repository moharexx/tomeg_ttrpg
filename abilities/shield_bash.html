Shield Bash

Requirements:

  • Skill: 10 Melee Weapon

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Debuff, Mobility
  • Main-Action

Description

When you use this Ability, you Dash and Bash your Shield into a Target.
The Bash has Reach equal to your Natural Melee Reach, and the Dash can be made before or after the Bash.
The Target must make a STR Save of 8 × ESS; on a Fail they get the Dazed Condition.
The Dazed Condition lasts till End of Target's Next Turn.

Using this Ability with a Heavy Shield:

  • The STR Save increases to 12 × ESS, but The Dash Distance reduces to MS / 2
