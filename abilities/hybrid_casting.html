Hybrid Casting

Requirements:

  • Innate: 5 INT, 5 WILL, 5 EGO

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Utility
  • Buff, Soul Save

Description

Your CS can be INT + WILL + EGO, Capped at Total XP / 10
Your Soul Save Modifier can be (INT + WILL + EGO) / 2, Capped at Total XP / 20
When using the Memorization, Flow, and Wishing features you must still only use the specified Stat written!

You can choose 1 feature that you already have Unlocked; Memorization | Flow | Wishing
The chosen feature's specified Stat can be replaced by your regular CS, but the other features are disabled.
You can swap, or un-choose the specific feature when you take a Long-Rest.
