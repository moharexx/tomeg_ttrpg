Glacial Storm

Requirements:

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Offense
  • Ranged, Static Sphere AOE, VIT Save, Cold Damage, Crowd Control
  • Main-Action / Continuous

Description

Within Range, around a chosen point, the temperature drops, snow and ice grows and swirls.
When using this Ability, you are put in Glacial Storm Stance.
When Entering this Stance and in your subsequent Turns, Entities in the Radius must Succeed a VIT Save or take Damage.
Targets Damaged by this Ability get the Cold Effect.

Overflow (Main-Action):

  • For the rest of the Stance's Duration, its Radius is Doubled.
  • This feature can't Stack.

Cold Effect:

  • The Target's MS is reduced by CS^(1/3)
  • Slow Strength is Doubled against Soaked Targets.
  • Targets reduced to 0 MS have the Rooted Condition.
  • The Cold Effect Ends at the End of Target's Next Turn.

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
