Combat Teleport

Requirements:

  • Spell: 20 CS, (10 Transmutation | 20 Conjuration | 50 Enchantment)

Unlock Cost:

  • 20 XP

Tags:

  • Utility, Cooldown
  • Static Sphere AOE, Soul Save, Mobility
  • Main-Action + Bonus-Action

Description

When used, you choose Targets in a Radius around you to be Teleported to a Pinpointed location within Range.
Unwilling Targets can throw a Soul Save against being Teleported.
The Targets' collective weight can be up to 250 × ESS Kg
Where each Teleported Target end up in the destination's Radius is determined by the Caster.
Combat Teleport's Teleportation does NOT Activate Reactions, or Trigger Stances.

Note: Yes, you can exclude yourself from the Target list.
