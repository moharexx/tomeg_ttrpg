Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Oblivion Warp Stance.
On Stance Trigger; Entities within the Radius must make a Soul Save or Take 2d6 × ESS Demat Damage and get Teleported.
Teleported Entities get Teleported into a Pinpointed position, they are all position as close as possible to the Targeted position.
You can decide which Entity takes which closest possible position.
Oblivion Warp's Teleported does NOT Activate Reactions, or Trigger Stances.

The Stance Breaks when: Using Abilities, Moving

A spell forged in the most brutals of Elemental Battlegrounds.
