Way Of The Wanderer

Requirements:

  • Innate: 10 WILL
  • Skill: 20 Melee Weapon | 20 Martial Arts

Unlock Cost:

  • 10 XP

Tags:

  • Utility
  • Buff
  • Bonus-Action / Free-Action

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Way Of The Wanderer Stance.
While in this Stance, your CRIT Range is Increased by 1
On Stance Trigger your CRIT Range is instead Increased by 2 till the End of Your Turn.

The CRIT Range is only Effective on Actions with ESS (or Tier) not higher than 2× this Ability's, for Anti-Exploit reasons.
Note: After the Trigger you aren't in the Stance, and can get into another Stance without losing the CRIT Range Bonus.

The Stance only Breaks when entering any other Stance.
