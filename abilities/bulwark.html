Bulwark

Requirements:

  • Innate: 20 VIT
  • Skill: 5 Martial Arts | 10 Melee Weapon

Unlock Cost:

  • 5 XP

Tags:

  • Defense
  • Self, Negation, Buff
  • Free-Action / Continuous

Description

When using this Ability, you are put in Bulwark Stance.
While in Bulwark Stance, you can provide Cover for yourself and other Entities.
When you use the Defense-Action, you can Take Cover as a Free-Action in that Turn.
The Cover you provide is gone when you: Move or get moved.

Incoming Abilities with more than Double the ESS of this Ability are too powerful, and can ignore this Cover.

The Stance Breaks only when entering another Stance.
